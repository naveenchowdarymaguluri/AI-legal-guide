Transparency: What We Can and Cannot Guarantee

Intellectual integrity demands explicit delineation. We believe that overstating AI capabilities is an ethical violation in jurisprudence.

What We Guarantee

  • 100% Ephemeral Inference Enclaves: Your data is evaluated solely in isolated RAM segments and wiped upon session close.
  • Zero Training on Private Inputs: User documents, search syntax, and annotations are permanently ring-fenced from model weights.
  • Instant User-Directed Deletion: Clicking delete prompts true cryptographic erasure across every replica and vector store.
  • Cryptographic Citation Provenance: Every synthesis statement cites the verifiable excerpt or statute reference with zero hallucinated authorities.

What We Cannot Guarantee

  • Local Device Immune Defense: We cannot protect document security if an endpoint, browser extension, or client computer is compromised.
  • Courtroom Enforceability: Jurisdictional outcomes and contractual validity hinge upon individual judicial rulings and sovereign legislation.
  • Replacement of Qualified Counsel: LegalAI is an analytical intelligence platform designed to augment, not supplant, qualified attorney review and judgment.
  • Compromised Shared Credentials: Failure to enforce client-side MFA or using shared credentials compromises tenant isolation at the user tier.
