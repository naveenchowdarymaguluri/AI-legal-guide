psychology AI Legal Plain-Language Explanation

Under Section 25F, any employee meeting the statutory “workman” definition with continuous service of 1+ year cannot be terminated without:

  • (1) One month formal written notice detailing explicit grounds, OR full 1 month wage payout in lieu of notice;
  • (2) Retrenchment compensation equivalent to 15 days average pay for every completed year of continuous service.
