Frequently Asked Questions

document_scanner

How does document processing work?

Documents are ingested through high-precision OCR, tokenized, and processed strictly within your isolated private cryptographic session. No data is ever used to train public or proprietary AI models.

lock

Are my documents private?

Yes. LegalAI employs client-side AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. You hold exclusive cryptographic authority over your workspace with strict ephemeral execution enclaves.

delete_forever

Can I delete my documents?

Yes, at any time. Clicking delete permanently purges source files, generated embeddings, and all session logs from all systems immediately with no residual secondary backups.

gavel

Does LegalAI provide legal advice?

No. LegalAI is strictly an informational and analytical assistance platform designed to organize facts, highlight clauses, and cite statutory precedents. It is not a law firm and does not provide formal legal representation.

library_books

What sources are used for legal research?

We index official government gazettes, codified national and state statutes, verified appellate court precedents, and authoritative regulatory registers with verbatim line citations and verifiable document hashes.
