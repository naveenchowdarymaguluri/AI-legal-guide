Section 8 — Termination of Employment

TechCorp Operating Procedures and Departure Framework

8.1

Termination for Cause. The Employer may terminate the Employee's employment at any time with immediate effect, without any severance compensation or notice period, if the Employee commits gross negligence, willful misconduct, felony conviction, or material uncured breach of the terms stated in Section 6 (Confidentiality).

§ 8.2 TERMINATION WITHOUT CAUSE Asymmetric Notice Flag

Termination Without Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon delivery of written notice. The Employee shall provide not less than sixty (60) calendar days' advance written notice to TechCorp. The Employer may terminate at its sole discretion upon providing thirty (30) calendar days' written notice or equivalent salary in lieu thereof.

8.3

Severance Entitlements & Equity Acceleration. In the event of Termination Without Cause by the Employer under Section 8.2, Employee shall be entitled to receive continuation of Base Salary for six (6) months, standard health benefit subsidies (COBRA), and immediate 25% accelerated vesting of all unvested stock options under the 2024 TechCorp Omnibus Incentive Plan.

8.4

Post-Termination Obligations. Upon separation for any reason, Employee agrees to immediately deliver to TechCorp all company-issued hardware, encryption keys, confidential memoranda, and work product, in full compliance with the Surrender of Property Clause.
